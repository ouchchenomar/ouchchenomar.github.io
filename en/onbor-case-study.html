Case study

Onbor, turning fragmented manual operations into a data-driven business platform

Onbor is an internal counterparty onboarding platform for a trading floor. It covers case creation, multi-actor validations, document checks, contracting, electronic signatures, KPI reporting, and a full data governance layer.

  • Attijariwafa Bank - final-year internship 2026
  • FastAPI, React, PostgreSQL, Power BI
  • Workflow, governance, analytics, and data quality

Starting problem The process relied heavily on emails, Excel files, and PDFs, with limited visibility on the real status of each case and a high operational burden.
Goal Centralize the workflow, make controls more reliable, reduce manual work, and provide actionable KPIs to each role.
  • 5 Power BI dashboards
  • 10 Airflow DAGs
  • RBAC and audit trail
  • MDM and data quality governance

Architecture

A layered platform built around business usage

  • Backend: FastAPI, async SQLAlchemy, Pydantic, and Alembic migrations.
  • Frontend: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Tailwind, and Chart.js.
  • Document storage: MinIO for business documents and signed files.
  • E-signature: DocuSeal for signature requests and webhook integration.
  • Orchestration: Airflow for KPI pipelines, checks, documentation, and monitoring.
  • Database: PostgreSQL structured with business, governance, and analytics schemas.

Workflow

A traceable multi-actor progression

  • Draft, Front Office review, manager validation, and risk review.
  • Document verification and market or credit approvals.
  • Contracting, client signature, and authorized signatory signature.
  • Back-office setup, CTN setup, MLC configuration, and MCM introduction.
  • Critical transitions are role-bound and fully logged through workflow events.

Data and governance

Quality is built into the product, not added afterward

One of the strongest aspects of the project is how business experience and governance mechanisms are tied together.

Data quality and compliance

Completeness, consistency, and uniqueness checks, document compliance logic, deduplication, and runtime validation controls.

MDM and source-of-truth logic

Counterparty master data, historical snapshots, a data catalog, a business dictionary, and governed runtime parameters.

Role-based analytics

Analytics are not generic. Each role sees only the operational surfaces relevant to its work.

MLOps layer

The project also includes an MLOps layer with four prediction targets, monitoring, drift detection, and a model registry.
